Frequently asked questions

If your question isn't here, it takes about thirty seconds to ask us on WhatsApp.

We keep this site as a catalogue so you can see every design, its details and pricing clearly, without a checkout flow to maintain ourselves. Purchases route to our Meesho storefront, which already handles secure payments, buyer protection and order tracking — or you can order directly by messaging us.
Open any product page and tap "Buy on Meesho" — it takes you to that product's listing where you can check out normally. If you'd rather order through us directly, tap "Enquire on WhatsApp" or use the Contact page, and we'll confirm the order with you.
Yes — our gold-plated pieces use a nickel-safe base and micron gold plating, which is gentler on sensitive skin than standard imitation jewelry. We still recommend keeping pieces dry and removing them before swimming or showering to extend the plating's life.
Each ring's product page lists the available sizing, and most of our bands have a slight adjustable gap. If you're unsure which size to pick, message us your usual ring size on WhatsApp before ordering and we'll confirm the right fit.
Orders placed on Meesho follow Meesho's standard return and exchange policy for the seller, visible at checkout on the listing itself. Orders placed directly with us through Contact are handled case by case — message us within 3 days of delivery if there's an issue.
