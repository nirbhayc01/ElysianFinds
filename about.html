A small catalogue, chosen carefully

Elysian Finds started as a way to share the jewelry we kept getting asked about — pieces that photograph well, wear comfortably, and don't lose their shine in a month.

Our story

Why a catalogue, not a store

Most jewelry sites bury a handful of good pieces inside thousands of near-identical listings. We do the opposite: every product on Elysian Finds has been picked by hand, worn or examined in person, and described honestly — down to the plating and stone type.

We route the actual purchase through our Meesho storefront, where secure payments, buyer protection and delivery tracking are already handled well. If you'd rather ask a question first, we're one WhatsApp message away.

What guides what we list

Picked, not dropshipped in bulk

Each design earns a place in the catalogue on its own — we'd rather list forty pieces we stand behind than four hundred we haven't checked.

Honest about materials

Gold-plated brass is described as gold-plated brass. We list plating, base metal and stone type on every product page — no vague "premium alloy" language.

A real person on the other end

Questions about sizing, occasion-fit or bulk orders go to an actual person on WhatsApp, usually within the day.

Fulfilment we don't have to fake

Checkout, payment and shipping run through Meesho's established platform, so you get buyer protection we couldn't offer alone.

Ready to look through the catalogue?

Start with what you need it for — everyday wear, a festival, or an event — and filter from there.
